Inbox Zeroish

    Rules

    Checked top to bottom; the first rule that matches decides. A rule with no condition on a dimension ignores it. Keep rules hold mail in the inbox; anything else names a folder.

    Try it

    Categories

    What the classifier chooses between. "Means" is the definition; "not" settles the cases it tends to get wrong. Changing these re-classifies mail on the next run.
